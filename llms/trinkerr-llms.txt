# Trinkerr

> Trinkerr is a Bengaluru, India algorithmic and social trading platform founded in 2021 by Manvendra Singh and Gaurav Agarwal, backed by Accel and India Quotient. Its mobile app (com.trinkerr.app) automates trades with ready-made algos crafted by industry experts.
